How to wear it

Two steps. That's it.

i

Dip a fingertip.

Press the warm pad of your finger into the cream — it'll lift away in one soft swipe. No brush needed; your skin warms the formula and unlocks the chrome.

ii

Press across the lid.

Pat from the inner corner outward. Layer once for a glaze, twice for full metallic, three times for the wedding-guest moment.
