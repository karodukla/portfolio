01 · The Science

Your cells run on NAD⁺. Aging runs it down.

NAD⁺ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is the coenzyme that powers cellular energy, DNA repair, and metabolic regulation in every living cell. By age 50, levels can decline by more than 50 percent — and the symptoms are familiar: lower energy, slower recovery, diminished resilience.

Oral precursors raise NAD⁺ steadily over weeks. Intravenous delivery bypasses digestion entirely, depositing NAD⁺ directly into the bloodstream at a bioavailability oral capsules cannot match.
